Jefflix Trailer
Short Name: jefflix
Completed Date: May 19, 2024
Medium: video
Course Info: n/a
Abstract: This project presents a high energy, entertainment themed promotional campaign video designed to showcase what it is like to create a yearbook and to drive book sales. Yearbook sales often struggle because students don’t realize the intense creative labor yearbook staff go through behind the scenes. By pitching a trailer concept that matches the theme of the yearbook, it allows people to see what it's like to be part of the creative process. It transforms a traditional sales pitch into an engaging moment that builds  and represents school pride.
