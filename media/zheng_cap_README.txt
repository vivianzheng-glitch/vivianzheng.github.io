The Synapse Cap
Short Name: cap
Completed Date: July 30, 2026
Medium: presentation
Course Info: MAT110CF, Nefeli Manoudaki
Abstract: This project presents a hat for your thoughts to come alive called the Synapse Cap. I created this for my final presentation in MAT110CF where we have to create a concept on what something could be. The idea for this cap came from me, wishing that I could see my ideas without doing the hard work. So I thought of what if anyone could plug their brain straight into a computer and bring any idea to life instantly. This presentation was made not only because it was an assignment for me to do but it allows me to question if people can bring their ideas to life without doing the hard work for it.
