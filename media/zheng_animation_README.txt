Dance Animation
Short Name: animation
Completed Date: July 2, 2026
Medium: animation video
Course Info: n/a
Abstract: This project presents a short form, frame by frame 2D digital animation that translates dynamic human choreography into visual animation media. It focuses on rhythm, fluidity, and bodily expression that uses animation to highlight the performance and energy. Each frame was manually drawn to capture the dancer's movement. This video was made as a gift for my friend whose birthday celebration I couldn’t physically attend. The goal was to solve a problem of distance and I wanted to create something that is highly personal to my friend that celebrates their unique identity.
