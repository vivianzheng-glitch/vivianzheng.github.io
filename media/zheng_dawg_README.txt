Abstract: This project presents a behind the scenes video of a short film called Don’t Trip. Don’t Trip is about three friends who go on a camping trip where a mean spirited prank backfires. The behind the scene video captures all the fun times that happened while being on set. This video was made to show the transparency of what it is like to be on a film set and it highlights the production itself as people on crew get recognized for their hard work.
